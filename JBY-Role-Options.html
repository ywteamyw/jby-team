Our TeamRole display — 5 options to compare

How can we show roles?

Five ways to let visitors filter or read the team by role, on top of the existing filter by office. Try each one below — they all drive the same sample cards so you can compare the feel, not the data.

Sample team of 8 with placeholder role grouping, purely to demo the UI. Real people, offices and Sales/Service assignments get wired in once we pull the full team.

01

Underline tabs

Same quiet, editorial style as the office filter. Scales to many roles and stays out of the way. This is what’s live now.

Role
02

Pill toggles

Rounded chips that fill in when active. Friendly and obviously tappable — reads instantly as a filter, great on mobile.

03

Dropdown

The most compact option. Hides choices until opened — best when the toolbar is tight or there are lots of roles.

Show
04

Segmented control

Connected buttons in one track, like a switch. Feels tactile and modern; best with a small, fixed set of roles.

05

Grouped sections

No filter at all — cards sit under role headings with a count each. Shows the whole team at a glance, the way the real About page groups people by office.
